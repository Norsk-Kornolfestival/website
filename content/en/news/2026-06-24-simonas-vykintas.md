Simonas and Vykintas are back! They were at the festivals in 2017 and
2018 and made a lot of friends there. We have asked them to come back
to demonstrate the brewing of Lithuanian _keptinis_. This is a beer
where the mash is baked in an oven, and Simonas and Vykintas will
demonstrate how it is brewed in the hall on the Saturday.

Simonas Gutautas is a [commercial brewer at the Dundulis
brewery](https://www.garshol.priv.no/blog/325.html), while Vykintas
Motuza is a traditional brewer out in the countryside.
