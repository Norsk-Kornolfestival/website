Friday at 1830 there will be a panel discussion (probably mostly in
Norwegian) with Anders Riise, province mayor for Møre og Romsdal,
Sølvi Dimmen, mayor of Volda county, Mika Laitinen, of Brewing Nordic
from Finland, and Terje Grønmy from Hanen, an organization for farm
food and tourism. They will be talking about the future of traditional
beer in the context of the proposed new law for sale of alcohol
directly from producer.

Vidar Skeie will introduce the discussion with remarks on the status
for having farmhouse brewing listed as UNESCO immaterial cultural
heritage.
