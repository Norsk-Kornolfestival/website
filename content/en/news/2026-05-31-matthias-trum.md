Matthias Trum will be at the festival this year to do a talk. He is
the owner of the Schlenkerla brewery in Bamberg in Germany,
world-famouse for their smoked beer. Smoked beer used to be seen as
strange, old-fashioned, and almost undrinkable, but is today
recognized around the world as a cultural treasure and high-quality
beer. The Schlenkerla brewery have been a very important part of
this development.

Matthias's talk will be held in English, and he describes it like
this:

*Smokebeer: A Historical Journey and Future Prospects*

Over the past two centuries, traditionally crafted smoked beer - or
Rauchbier, as it is known in Germany - has become a rarity in the
world of brewing. In his presentation, Matthias Trum, sixth-generation
owner and brewmaster of the historic Schlenkerla smokebeer brewery,
explores the fascinating history behind this unique beer style and the
broader evolution of beer culture. He will delve into the reasons for
smokebeer's transformation into a unique specialty, while also
offering insights into the current landscape of Rauchbier -
particularly within the vibrant beer diversity and traditions of
modern Franconia and Bavaria. Finally, the presentation will reflect
on how this smokey brewing heritage can be preserved and celebrated in
an era of rapid sociocultural transformation.
