Mika Laitinen, ein av dei mest kjende skribentane om gardsøl, har vore
på festivalen fleire gonger tidlegare, og vi er glade for å kunne
kunngjere at han kjem attende i år. Han skal halde eit foredrag om ein
type gjæra gardsdrykk som nokså ufortent har gått i gløymeboka.

Foredraget blir halde på engelsk, så omtalen er òg på engelsk.

**The Farmer's Daily Cereal Drinks**

The farmhouse folks of the past in the east drank large amounts of
cereal beverages daily. These daily brews involved alcoholic and
lactic fermentation, but they weren't intoxicating. The daily drinks
were food for the whole family. Names like kalja, kali, gira, kvass,
and taari echo this past. Today, these drinks are rare or have been
replaced by more modern sugar-boosted versions. This talk sheds light
on the mysteries of old-time daily cereal drinks. Why did the farmers
brew them? Are they worth brewing today? What did these drinks taste
like?  How to brew them yourself?
